## Recommended Submission

- **Track:** Demo
- **Topic:** Collective Intelligence (CI), with HCOMP secondary
- **Title:** Collective Intelligence in Agile Teams: A Transparent Simulation Testbed for Studying Team Decisions Across Sprints

## How the two papers divide the work

- **Poster** (`POSTER_SUBMISSION.md` / `.tex`) — the *conceptual* article: why collective intelligence is the right lens for agile teamwork, grounded with inline citations. Scrum is introduced *in the text*, not the title.
- **Demo** (`DEMO_SUBMISSION.md`) — the *tool* paper: architecture (Figure 1) and operation (Figures 2–4), with a short conceptual framing that points to the poster.
